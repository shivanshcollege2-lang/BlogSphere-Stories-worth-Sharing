I’m excited to share **BlogSphere**, a full-stack blogging platform I built for my academic module submission.

BlogSphere gives readers a calm, editorial space to discover stories and gives writers a simple way to publish and manage their work. The project includes searchable and category-filtered posts, user accounts, author profiles, and complete blog CRUD with ownership checks.

**Built with:** React, Vite, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Mongoose and JWT-based authentication.

This project gave me a chance to bring frontend experience, API design, database modeling and authentication together in one application. I’m looking forward to continuing to improve it.
